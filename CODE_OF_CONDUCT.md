# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and safe
experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background,
or point of view.

## Our standards

Contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
  and other project spaces.
- Give and accept constructive feedback without personal attacks.
- Assume good intent, ask clarifying questions, and disagree in good faith.
- Focus on the technical merits of proposals and keep discussions scoped to the
  project.
- Respect different levels of familiarity with Stellar, Soroban, and this
  codebase.
- Avoid posting private data, credentials, or details of unpatched security
  issues in public channels. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, or derogatory language.
- Threats of violence, intimidation, or targeted disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, inflammatory remarks, or persistent disruption of discussions.
- Misrepresenting affiliation with the project or its maintainers.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an
  investigation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including repository
issues, discussions, pull requests, code reviews, and any official project
communication where a person is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not open a public issue for a conduct
report. Include what happened, where it happened, and any relevant context.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports; follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use GitHub private vulnerability reporting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports promptly and may take any action they consider
appropriate, including a private warning, public correction, removal of
content, temporary restriction, or permanent ban. Maintainers who do not follow
or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face the same consequences.
